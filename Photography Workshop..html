what you'll learn

From fundamentals to finishing.

Each workshop or mentoring session is built around these core pillars — so you leave with skills, not just notes.

01

Composition & Framing

How to see a frame before you lift the camera — rule of thirds, leading lines, negative space, and when to break every rule.

02

Lighting Fundamentals

Understanding natural and artificial light — how to read it, shape it, and use it to create depth, mood, and dimension in every image.

03

Camera Mechanics

Manual mode, aperture, shutter speed, ISO — not just what they do, but why you'd choose one over the other in real shooting situations.

04

Post-Processing

Editing with intention — color grading, retouching, and finishing your images in a way that feels consistent and professional, not overdone.
