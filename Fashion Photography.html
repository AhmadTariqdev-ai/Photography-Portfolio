the craft

Four fashion lighting signatures.

Each look is crafted with a specific light setup that flatters fabric, form, and attitude.

Loop

Soft, flattering shadow loop — ideal for beauty and editorial portraits.

Butterfly

High-key, glamorous — creates a soft shadow under the nose, perfect for high-fashion.

Split

Dramatic half-light, half-shadow — bold, edgy, and editorial.

Rembrandt

Classic, painterly triangle on the cheek — moody and timeless.
