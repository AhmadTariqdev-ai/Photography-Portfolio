the craft

Four ways we shape the light.

Every session borrows from a handful of classic lighting patterns, chosen for your face and the mood you're after.

Rembrandt

A triangle of light on the shadow cheek — moody, dimensional, classic.

Butterfly

Light straight above the face casts a soft wing-shaped shadow under the nose.

Split

Light from directly to the side, dividing the face evenly in light and shadow.

Loop

A small shadow loop beside the nose — flattering, subtle, everyday.
